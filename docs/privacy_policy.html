Privacy Policy

FastTrack is an intermittent-fasting, weight, and hydration tracker built by Kodevengers. This page explains, plainly, what the app does with your information — which is very little, because almost none of it goes anywhere.

In short

  • Your fasting logs, weight, and photos are stored only on your phone, in a local database.
  • FastTrack has no servers, no account, and no sign-in — we never receive your data at all.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.
  • Delete everything anytime with Reset App Data in Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

01 Information we collect

FastTrack collects information only through what you choose to enter while using the app. None of it is transmitted anywhere — it's read and written directly on your device.

DataWhere it's enteredStored as
Fasting sessionsTimer start/end, adjusted timesLocal database
Weight entriesProfile weight logLocal database
Water intakeHydration trackerLocal preferences
Display name & photoProfile screenLocal file storage
Goals & preferencesOnboarding, SettingsLocal preferences

We don't ask for your real name, email, date of birth, or any government-issued ID, and the app has no field that requests them.

02 How your data is used

Everything you enter is used for exactly one purpose: showing it back to you. Your fasting history draws your streaks and charts; your weight log draws your progress curve; your goal and experience answers shape the suggested protocol and default reminders. None of this leaves the device to be "used" by us in any other sense — there is no analytics pipeline receiving it, and no model or service we train or improve with it.

03 Permissions we request

Notifications

Used only to deliver the reminders you turn on yourself — fast start/end alerts, metabolic stage milestones, and water reminders. Every one of these is scheduled locally by the app; nothing is sent through a push-notification server.

Camera & photo library

Requested only if you choose to set a profile picture. The image is copied into the app's private storage on your device and is never uploaded.

04 Storage & security

FastTrack stores data using standard on-device mechanisms: a local SQLite database for fasting and weight history, and the operating system's private app-preferences store for settings and goals. Both are sandboxed by iOS/Android to this app alone — other apps can't read them, and neither can we, since the app has no network code that would send them to us.

05 Sharing with third parties

We don't share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose your data, because we don't have it to share. FastTrack contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting service that phones home. There is no third party in this picture at all.

06 Retention & deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep the app installed. You're in full control of it:

  • Delete a single fasting or weight entry with a swipe, right from History or Profile.
  • Settings → Reset App Data permanently erases all logs on the spot.
  • Uninstalling FastTrack removes its entire local database and every stored preference.

Because we never receive a copy, there's no server-side record for us to delete on your behalf — removing it on your device removes it, full stop.

07 Children's privacy

FastTrack is not directed at children and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). As the app collects no data from us in the first place, this is naturally the case regardless of the user's age.

08 Your choices

Since your information exists only on your device, most privacy rights you'd normally request from a company — access, correction, export, deletion — are things you already have directly in the app: every screen that shows your data lets you edit or remove it yourself, and Settings → Reset App Data covers the rest in one tap.

09 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version of FastTrack adds an optional cloud-backup feature — we'll update this page and revise the effective date above. We won't quietly start collecting more than what's described here without updating this document first.
